What this preview stores

When you register, we store your name, email address, password hash, and your confirmation that you are 21 or older. We store login sessions, sign-in and sign-out events, and failed login identifiers as hashes to help protect accounts. Passwords are not stored in plain text.

When signed in, your visited page paths, cart choices, quantities, quiz results, mock orders, demo delivery details, and referral attribution are saved in the database. Form submissions, including contact and creator applications, are also stored. We do not record keystrokes, payment cards, or the contents of password fields in activity logs.

Who can access your information

You can access your own bag and orders. Authorized Reboot administrators can review orders, activity, and submissions, and export sales records. This preview does not send marketing emails or use advertising trackers.

Retention and requests

Preview data is retained until the operator removes it. Use the contact form with “Privacy request” as the subject to request access, correction, or deletion. Please use sample delivery information when testing.

{% elif page == 'terms' %}

A preview experience

This website previews Reboot, ALDICOM’s US line. All listed prices are sample prices in US dollars. Checkout is a simulation. A mock order does not create a purchase, take payment, or trigger shipment.

Accounts and use

Account registration is for adults aged 21 or older. Keep your credentials private. Customer accounts can save a bag and place mock orders. Administrator access is restricted to authorized operators.

Product information

Product concepts, artwork, flavors, and packaging may change. Final labels will provide complete ingredient, nutrition, and allergen information. This preview makes no health, treatment, or performance claims.

Creator participation

Submitting an application does not establish a partnership. Any future sponsored content must clearly disclose the relationship and follow the brand’s confirmed content guidelines.

{% else %}

Essential cookies

We use a session cookie to support sign-in, form security, and flash notifications. If you choose “Keep me signed in,” a remember cookie can keep your session available for up to 30 days. Logging out revokes that session.

Referral attribution

Opening a creator link sets a ref_code cookie for 30 days. The latest valid link determines the preview discount and order attribution. Cart and order records are stored in the database, not in this cookie.

Display preferences

A local browser preference remembers whether you paused motion. It contains no account or shopping data. There are no advertising or third-party analytics cookies in this implementation.
